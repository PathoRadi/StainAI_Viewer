Making microglial analysis simple,
from whole-slide quantification to specific regions.

StainAI-Web classifies, quantifies, and visualizes microglial morphology in immunohistochemistry images, such as Iba1-stained sections. The platform converts raw microglial images into AI-generated morphology maps, allowing users to select regions of interest (ROIs) and generate quantitative data for whole-slide and regional analysis.

Core Functionality

Background Correction

Reduces uneven illumination and staining artifacts to enhance cellular signals.

Whole Slide Inference

Analyzes ultra-high-resolution images using overlap-aware patch-based inference.

Morphology Classification

Detects and classifies microglia into six morphological types across both gray and white matter.

Interactive Visualization

Provides high-resolution navigation and morphology-resolved image overlays.

ROI Quantification

Quantifies cell counts, morphology distributions, and densities within user-defined regions.

Structured Data Export

Exports detection results, quantitative data, and ROI information for downstream analysis.
